# Asset disposal

The disposal module permanently removes serialized assets or lot-controlled quantities through a formal `DisposalProcess`. Every process records its organization, optional exact unit, process number, reason, operator, items and immutable stock snapshots. A process may also contain one `Destruction` record with method, date and certificate.

Finalization is atomic. Individual assets change from `AVAILABLE` to `DISPOSED`. Lot quantities reduce both `StockBalance.available` and `StockLot.availableQuantity`. Every item creates one negative `DISPOSAL` stock movement. Destruction fields are optional as a group and must all be supplied when physical destruction is recorded.

Canonical UUID request identifiers provide idempotent retries, pessimistic locks protect concurrent operations, and failures roll back the complete process. Process numbers are unique within an organization. The `disposals` resource supports `READ`, `CREATE` and `APPROVE` at SYSTEM, ORGANIZATION and UNIT scope. Finalization requires both `CREATE` and `APPROVE`, including each item's scope, and an explicit `confirmed: true` request field. Existing integrations must send this field; existing operator profiles need the additional approval grant. Revocation is checked on retries too. Production must enable both `ERP_REQUIRE_LOGIN` and `ERP_ENFORCE_PERMISSIONS`; the existing unprotected development mode is not an authorization boundary.

Logical disposal is terminal removal from stock (`DISPOSED`), even without a destruction certificate. Temporary inactivation uses `BLOCKED` and does not create a disposal or attest physical destruction. Physical destruction adds method, timestamp and certificate to the terminal disposal. Registration, inspections, new occurrences and resolution of pre-existing recovery occurrences cannot change a disposed asset back into use.

Only available individual assets are eligible: active custody, maintenance and reservation prevent disposal. A stock balance with reserved quantities cannot be disposed until the reservation is released. Locations with `OPEN` or `COUNTED` inventory counts are excluded from the picker and rejected at finalization, for assets and lots. Inventory creation and disposal serialize through the organization lock. Transfers in this repository finalize atomically, without an active/in-transit stage; a transferred asset can no longer be disposed from its former unit. No new transfer lifecycle is introduced here.

The browser shows the items, scope, quantities and destruction data for confirmation before submitting. Cancel sends nothing. Retrying an uncertain result preserves the request UUID and confirmed payload. History explicitly distinguishes logical disposal from certified destruction. See [integrated validation](armamento-015-validation.md).

API endpoints are `GET /api/erp/disposals/stock`, `POST /api/erp/disposals`, `GET /api/erp/disposals` and `GET /api/erp/disposals/{id}`. The English frontend is available at `/erp/disposals`.
